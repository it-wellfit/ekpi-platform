# ADR-NNNN: Short decision title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Rejected | Superseded by ADR-NNNN | Deprecated
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Owner:** Role or named accountable person
- **Deciders:** Roles or names who approved
- **Related:** requirement/decision IDs, issue, PR, contract, ADR

## Context

Describe the forces that make a decision necessary: the problem, constraints, current behavior, affected users/components, risk tier and any deadline. State facts that can be checked. Link the evidence or command that established them.

Do not write a solution here.

## Decision

State the choice in one or two sentences, in the active voice: “We will …”. Include the scope and the explicit non-goals. If the decision has conditions or an expiry, state them.

## Consequences

### Positive

- What becomes easier, safer or cheaper.

### Negative / accepted cost

- What becomes harder, slower or more constrained.
- What residual risk is accepted, by whom, and until when.

### Follow-up

- Migrations, compatibility notes, documentation, tests, monitoring, training or cleanup required.
- Links to issues/tickets. Use `Pending` plus an owner for anything not yet done; do not imply it is complete.

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Why rejected |
| --- | --- |
| … | … |

Include “do nothing” when it is a real option.

## Evidence

- Command/test/drill and result, with revision and date.
- Review or approval record.
- If evidence is absent, state `Not run` and the owner who must produce it. Never imply an unexecuted check passed.

## Compliance and security impact

- Does this change a trust boundary, data classification, retention, access control, audit evidence or a public contract?
- Which control/decision ID is affected?
- Is a security review or data-owner approval required?

## Supersession

- Supersedes: ADR-NNNN or none.
- Superseded by: ADR-NNNN or none.
- If superseded, update the old record's status and link; do not rewrite its decision.
